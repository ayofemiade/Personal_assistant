Moyo: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Operated by Moyosore Ayofemi-Adetoye (MoyoCode Technologies) · Contact: oreayofemi@gmail.com

What Moyo is

Moyo is a private, personal productivity application that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public. It connects only to accounts belonging to its owner.

Data Moyo accesses

  • TikTok (via TikTok Login Kit and Display API, read-only): basic profile information, follower count, and the owner's public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Instagram (via the Instagram API, read-only): the owner's posts and their likes, comments, views and follower count.
  • Google (Gmail and Google Calendar, read-only): calendar events, plus the sender and subject of recent emails. Email content is retrieved only when the owner opens a specific email and is not stored.

How the data is used

Solely to show the owner their own schedule, inbox summary and content performance inside Moyo, and to generate personal planning suggestions. Moyo never posts, comments, messages, deletes or changes anything on any connected account.

Storage and security

Data is stored locally on the owner's computer. Access tokens are kept in the operating system's credential manager. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. When an AI service is used to generate suggestions, client names and amounts are replaced with placeholders before anything leaves the computer, and email/calendar content is not sent.

Retention and deletion

Synced email metadata is kept for up to 14 days. Disconnecting an account inside Moyo revokes access and deletes the locally synced copies. Access can also be revoked at any time from TikTok, Instagram/Meta or Google account settings.
